

- **The quaternion multiplication table** — how the basis elements 1, i, j, k multiply, and why the product is non-commutative (ij = k but ji = −k).
- **Visualizing multiplication as a geometric operation** — thinking of quaternion multiplication not just as an algebraic rule but as combined rotation + scaling in 4D, often shown via diagrams of how basis vectors map to each other.
- **The relationship between quaternions and complex numbers** — quaternions as a 4D extension of the 2D complex plane, and how sub-algebras of quaternions behave like copies of the complex numbers.
- **Conjugation, norm, and inverses** — geometric meaning of the quaternion conjugate and how it relates to distance/length in 4D space.
- **Group structure** — how unit quaternions form a group under multiplication, setting up later chapters on how this connects to rotations in 3D (the double-cover relationship with SO(3)).
